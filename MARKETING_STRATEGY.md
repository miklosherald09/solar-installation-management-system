# WorkWorkWork — Marketing Strategy

**Prepared by:** A mid-range employee (working from home)
**Based in:** Sta. Barbara, Pangasinan, Philippines
**Product:** WorkWorkWork — a two-sided mobile marketplace ("Find work. Hire near you.")
that connects people who need everyday jobs done (cleaning, gardening, pet sitting,
car wash, repair, moving, driver, painting, construction) with local, verified workers.
Includes in-app voice/video calling, real-time chat, geolocation ("Near Me"),
and ratings/credibility profiles.

> This document is a practical, executable strategy built for **one person with a
> mid-range salary, no big budget, and a home office in Sta. Barbara**. It prioritizes
> low-cost, high-leverage, local-first actions that a solo marketer can actually run.

## 1. Executive Summary

WorkWorkWork is a two-sided marketplace: **it only works when there are enough
workers AND enough job posters in one place.** The #1 risk in a small town like
Sta. Barbara is the "empty app" problem — a user downloads it, sees no one nearby,
and never returns.

So the strategy is **supply-first and local-only**:

1. Seed a critical mass of **workers (supply)** in Sta. Barbara before any paid ads.
2. Use **proof-based, low-cost local marketing** (Facebook groups, barangay networks,
   word-of-mouth referrals) to trigger demand.
3. Keep the funnel **metered and measurable**, so effort goes where it produces jobs.
4. Only expand to Calasiao → Urdaneta → Dagupan after Sta. Barbara reaches a defined
   quality threshold (match rate, response time, ratings).

Because you are a solo mid-range marketer working from home, this plan relies on
**consistency and free/cheap channels**, not on a large advertising budget.

## 3. Positioning & Target Audience

### 3.1 Positioning Statement

> **For** homeowners in Sta. Barbara (and nearby Pangasinan towns) who need reliable,
> on-demand help for everyday jobs,
> **WorkWorkWork is** the local job marketplace that connects them directly with
> verified workers in the neighborhood,
> **unlike** scrolling through scattered Facebook posts,
> **because** you can see nearby pros on a map, check ratings, chat, and literally
> call them in-app — no middleman.

### 3.2 Two Audiences (two-sided market)

**Audience A — Workers / Pros (SUPPLY side)**

- Karpintero, mason, pintor
- Tubero, elektrisyan, aircon tech
- Hakot / lipat-bahay / driver / delivery
- Tagalinis ng bahay, labandera, hardinero, pet sitter
- People who want _dagdag na raket_ (extra income) without agency fees.

**Audience B — Job Posters / Clients (DEMAND side)**

- Homeowners & renters (household repairs, cleaning)
- Small business owners (delivery, hauling)
- Seniors / busy families / OFW families who need help now.

### 3.3 Core Messages (keep consistent everywhere)

- **For workers:** _Sagot ka! Direktang tatawag sa'yo ang customer. Walang bawas sa kita. Protektado ang number mo._
- **For clients:** _May kailangan kang gawin sa bahay? Hanapin agad ang verified pro malapit sa'yo — tawag agad, walang tanong-tanong sa Facebook._
- **Brand line (already in assets):** _"Find work. Hire near you."_

## 4. Strategy Pillars

1. **Supply-first seeding** — get workers onboarded before demand campaigns.
2. **Hyper-local trust** — leverage your Sta. Barbara presence: barangay captains,
   church/community groups, local Facebook pages.
3. **Proof over ads** — use before/after photos, video testimonials, and "real job
   completed" content. Trust is your cheapest conversion tool.
4. **Direct "near me" relevance** — every ad and post emphasizes _Sta. Barbara / kalapit-bayan_.
5. **Measured, phased expansion** — only expand geography after local quality gates pass.
6. **Retention via referrals** — GCash-friendly referral loop for both workers and clients.

             signup      match      rebook      neighbor

Metrics per stage:
  Installs         Signups /       Match rate    30-day return   Referral

**North-star metric (for a marketplace):** **Weekly Matched Jobs** — the number of jobs
posted that get a real call/chat and a completed outcome. This single number tells you
whether the marketplace is alive. Base every campaign decision on it.

## 6. Channels & Tactics

### 6.1 Free / Organic (run these first, every week)

| Channel                                         | Tactic                                                                                                                                                            | Cadence        |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------- |
| **Facebook Groups**                             | Post in Sta. Barbara / Pangasinan Buy & Sell, hire, and community groups (with admin permission). Create your own "WorkWorkWork Sta. Barbara" group as home base. | 3–5 posts/week |
| **Facebook Page**                               | Post completed-job proof, tips, worker spotlights, testimonials.                                                                                                  | 3–4 posts/week |
| **Messenger / referral**                        | Share referral links; follow up with engaged users personally (you're local — you can do this).                                                                   | Daily 15 min   |
| **Barangay network**                            | Coordinate with barangay captains / kagawad to announce the app; ask to post posters at hall, sari-sari stores.                                                   | Monthly        |
| **Word-of-mouth**                               | Ask first 10 satisfied clients to tell one neighbor; offer referral bonus.                                                                                        | Ongoing        |
| **TikTok / Reels**                              | Short before/after + "how fast I found a worker" clips in Taglish.                                                                                                | 2–3/week       |
| **GCash communities / OFW family groups**       | OFW families who need someone to handle errands/repairs at home are ideal clients.                                                                                | Weekly         |
| **Classmates / colleagues / neighborhood chat** | Your existing network is your launch audience — use it.                                                                                                           | Launch week    |

### 6.2 Micro-Paid (only after supply is ready, keep ₱ low)

- **Facebook/Instagram Ads:** Meta ads targeting Sta. Barbara + 8 km, age 22–60.
- Two campaigns, tightly split:
  - _Workers:_ "Mag-sign up nang libre — walang bawas sa kita."
  - _Clients:_ "Mabilisang tulong sa bahay — direkta tawag."
- Start at **₱150–₱300/day** total, for 1–2 weeks, just to validate. Pause anything
  that doesn't produce installs under ₱25 (client) / ₱60 (pro).

### 6.3 Store/Listing Optimization (free but important)

- Keep the Google Play description and App Store copy updated with the **Sta. Barbara
  launch message** and current feature highlights (native iPhone calling, Near Me slider).
- Use local keywords in the listing: _home services Pangasinan, hire worker near me,
  dagdag kita, part-time work_.

## 7. 30-Day Local Launch Plan (Sta. Barbara)

> Load-bearing idea: **seed workers first, then turn on demand.** If a client opens the
> app and there's nobody to call, everything else is wasted.

### Week 0 (Prep — Days 1–3)

- [ ] Finalize the Sta. Barbara message + poster/creative (Canva, free).
- [ ] Create the "WorkWorkWork Sta. Barbara" Facebook group.
- [ ] Prepare a one-page "Founding Pro" pitch for workers (benefits: libre, badge, protektadong number).
- [ ] Set up a simple tracking sheet (install source, signup, jobs posted) in Google Sheets.

### Week 1 (Supply Seeding — Days 4–10)

**Goal:** Onboard **50–70 verified workers/pros in Sta. Barbara** before running ads.

- [ ] List target pros from your network: carpenters, plumbers, electricians, aircon techs,
      movers/hakot, cleaners, gardeners. Aim for a mix across the 6 high-demand categories.
- [ ] Offer a **"Founding Pro ng Sta. Barbara"** badge + 100% free period as incentive.
- [ ] Visit / message each candidate; help them set up a profile with a photo and 1–2 line pitch.
- [ ] Post the pro recruitment message in local Facebook groups.

### Week 2 (Demand Ignite — Days 11–17)

**Goal:** **80+ real job posts** from Sta. Barbara clients.

- [ ] Announce the app to clients once pros are visible on the map.
- [ ] Run a small FB test ad for **clients** (₱150/day, 5 days) targeting Sta. Barbara + 8 km.
- [ ] Personally seed 10–20 jobs yourself or with friends/family to prove liquidity.
- [ ] Document every completed job (photo + testimonial) for future content.

### Week 3 (Liquidity Engine — Days 18–24)

**Goal:** Median first response < **15 minutes**; match rate > **75%** within 1 hour.

- [ ] Follow up by Messenger with every job poster — personally, since you're local.
- [ ] Push notification/reactivation: _"May N verified pros na available sa Sta. Barbara ngayong weekend."_
- [ ] Roll out the referral loop (workers + clients).
- [ ] Start A/B testing ads: 2 client creatives, 2 worker creatives; pause losers.

### Week 4 (Viral & Review — Days 25–30)

**Goal:** **1,000 users**, > 4.6★ average, and a working referral engine.

- [ ] Ask every completed job to leave a rating & review.
- [ ] Share the 3 best success stories as social proof content.
- [ ] Measure the 5 expansion gates (see §11.3); if met, prepare Calasiao playbook.

## 11. Metrics & KPIs

### 11.1 North-Star

- **Weekly Matched Jobs** (jobs posted → someone calls/chats → completed)

### 11.2 Supporting metrics

| Metric                          | Target (Month 1 Sta. Barbara)                    |
| :------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------- |
| Active verified pros            | 60+ across barangays                             |
| Total registered users          | 1,000+                                           |
| Jobs posted / week              | 25–40                                            |
| Match rate (called within 1 hr) | > 75%                                            |
| Median time to first call       | < 12–15 min                                      |
| Avg. rating                     | > 4.6 / 5.0                                      |
| Blended CAC                     | < ₱25 per client install; < ₱60 per verified pro |
| Referral-driven signups         | > 30%                                            |

### 11.3 The 5 Expansion Gates (Sta. Barbara → Calasiao/Urdaneta)

1. **Match rate** > 75% for 7 straight days
2. **Median response** < 15 minutes
3. **Pro utilization** > 40% of pros get ≥1 gig/week
4. **Average rating** > 4.6★
5. **Organic word-of-mouth** > 30% of signups come from shared links

## 13. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk                                | Likelihood | Mitigation                                                                              |
| :---------------------------------- | :--------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Empty app (no supply near user)** | High       | Supply-first seeding; launch only after 50–70 pros live; seed demo jobs yourself.       |
| **No budget approval**              | High       | Prove results with micro-pilot (₱1,500/mo) + organic wins before requesting more.       |
| **User trust (strangers on app)**   | Medium     | Push ratings/reviews, "Verified" badges, in-app calling (numbers stay private).         |
| **Low engagement after launch**     | Medium     | Weekly reactivation push + personal Messenger follow-up (you're local — big advantage). |
| **Referral abuse / spam**           | Low        | Cap rewards, require completed jobs for bonuses, set clear terms.                       |
| **Expanding too fast**              | Medium     | Enforce the 5 expansion gates — never skip.                                             |
| **Time constraints (day job)**      | Medium     | Batch content, schedule posts, timebox outreach to 30 min/day.                          |

## 14. Weekly Review Ritual

> 30 minutes every Friday. The whole strategy is worthless without this.

1. Fill in the tracking sheet: installs, signups, jobs posted, matches, referrals.
2. Compute the north-star: **Weekly Matched Jobs** (up or down? why?).
3. Check each active campaign: install cost vs. the < ₱25 / < ₱60 targets.
4. Decide: **double down** on what worked, **cut** what didn't, **test** one new thing.
5. Log one "learned" note and one "next action" for the coming week.
