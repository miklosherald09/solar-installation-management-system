### Promotional text (full description)
```
WorkWorkWork — find work or hire trusted workers right in your neighborhood.

One app, two sides. Post a job in under a minute, or browse real work
opportunities near you and start earning today. No agencies, no middlemen —
just you and the people a few blocks away.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Post a job — Cleaning, gardening, pet sitting, car wash, repair, moving,
  driver, painting, construction, and more. Add photos, set your budget,
  pick a schedule, and choose where the work happens.
• Find work near you — Turn on "Near Me" to see only the jobs around you.
  Search by GPS, choose a spot on the map, or adjust the radius from 1 to
  100 km.
• Hire local pros — Compare helpers by rating, reviews, and completed jobs.
  Every worker has a credibility profile built from real results.
• Chat in real time — Agree on details, ask questions, and confirm the job
  in instant messaging.
• Call instantly — Voice and video calls make discussing a job as easy as
  calling a friend. On iPhone, incoming work calls ring natively like a
  regular phone call — even when the app is closed.
• Stay in the loop — Push notifications let you know the second a new job,
  a message, or a work request arrives.

WHY WORKWORKWORK
• Built for your neighborhood — local jobs, local people.
• Fair, transparent budgets — you post what it's worth; you accept what
  fits.
• Trust you can see — ratings, reviews, and credibility badges on every
  profile.
• Simple to start — sign in with Apple or Google, or try the demo account.
  Currency and location settings adapt automatically to your device.

Whether you need a helper for the weekend or you're looking for your next
gig, WorkWorkWork connects you with the work around you.

Download free and get to work.
```
