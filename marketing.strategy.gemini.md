# WorkWorkWork - Marketing & Growth Playbook
## Target Launch Market: **Sta. Barbara, Pangasinan**

A localized go-to-market execution strategy for **WorkWorkWork**, connecting local skilled workers/pros and clients in **Sta. Barbara, Pangasinan** (and adjacent areas like Calasiao, Urdaneta, and Dagupan) with real-time in-app calling (WebRTC/SIP), chat, and geolocation.

---

## Part 1: Strategic Foundation & The Local Supply-First Approach

In Pangasinan communities, word-of-mouth and Facebook Buy & Sell groups dominate local hiring, but finding available workers on short notice is frustrating. 

To win Sta. Barbara, **supply (workers/pros/laborers) must be seeded first**. A homeowner looking for an emergency plumber or aircon technician who opens the app and finds nobody will not return.

```
[ Phase 1: Sta. Barbara Core ] ──> [ Phase 2: Calasiao & Urdaneta Corridor ] ──> [ Phase 3: Greater Dagupan & Pangasinan ]
```

### 1. The Sta. Barbara Launch Radius (5–8 km)
- **Primary Launch Zone**: Sta. Barbara (Poblacion Norte/Sur, Minien, Tebag, Ventinilla, Maningding, Balingueo, Maticmatic, Nilombot).
- **Border Overflow**: Immediate borders of Calasiao and Urdaneta along the National Highway.
- **Why Sta. Barbara?**: Central Pangasinan hub connecting Dagupan, Calasiao, and Urdaneta. Dominating Sta. Barbara creates a bridgehead into the highest-density commercial zones in Pangasinan.

### Week 1: Supply Seeding (Days 1–7)
> **Goal**: Onboard **50–70 verified workers/pros** residing in Sta. Barbara before running any consumer ads.

#### 1. Target Pro Quotas
* **Karpintero / Mason / Pintor**: 15–20 pros
* **Aircon Tech / Elektrisyan / Tubero**: 15–20 pros
* **Hakot / Lipat-bahay / Delivery**: 10–15 pros
* **Cleaning / Labandera / Tabas Damo**: 10–15 pros

#### 2. The "Founding Pro" Incentive (Libre / Walang Bawas)
* **100% Free / Walang kaltas sa kita**: Zero commission or lead fees.
* **"Verified Pro ng Sta. Barbara" Badge**: Boosts trustworthiness.
* **Direct In-App Calling**: Protects personal contact numbers from being publicly spammed.
* **Early Access Top Placement**: Appear at the top of map & list results for the first 90 days.

#### 3. Ground & Digital Outreach in Sta. Barbara

* **Ground Action Hubs (6:30 AM – 9:00 AM)**:
  1. **Hardware & Construction Supply Stores**: Visit local hardware stores along McArthur Highway / Poblacion (e.g., local lumber yards, construction supply depots).
  2. **Sta. Barbara Public Market & Plaza**: Distribute laminated flyers with QR code directly to tricycle drivers (TODA terminals) and local service vendors.
  3. **Barangay Hall Bulletin Boards**: Coordinate with Barangay Captains/Kagawads to post notices for local livelihood opportunities.

* **Digital Facebook Outreach (Direct Messenger / SMS)**:
  * Search Facebook Marketplace & Pangasinan Buy & Sell groups for posts advertising aircon cleaning, plumbing, carpentry, or transport in Sta. Barbara/Calasiao.
  * **Taglish Outreach Script**:
    > *"Magandang araw Kuya/Boss [Pangalan]! Nakita ko po ang inyong post tungkol sa [Aircon Cleaning / Carpentry] service dito sa Sta. Barbara.
    > 
    > May bago po kaming app para sa Sta. Barbara at Pangasinan na tinatawag na **WorkWorkWork**. Dito po kayo direktang matatawagan at ma-message ng mga customer sa inyong barangay nang libre (walang bawas sa bayad sa inyo).
    > 
    > Libre po ang pag-join bilang 'Founding Pro' ngayong linggo. Kung gusto niyo po, send niyo lang sa akin ang 2-3 pictures ng gawa niyo at rates, ako na po ang magse-set up ng verified profile niyo: [Link]*"

#### 4. Concierge Onboarding ("Kami na ang Mag-aayos")
* Most local workers won't fill out long forms on their own.
* **Action**: Chat them on Messenger/SMS, collect their name, barangay, skillset, and past work photos, and create their profile on their behalf.

#### 5. Day-by-Day Checklist (Week 1)
* **Day 1–2**: List 150+ contacts from Facebook Marketplace, local posts, and hardware store leads in Sta. Barbara.
* **Day 3–4**: Send 50 Messenger/SMS invites per day. Visit 4 major TODA terminals and 3 hardware stores.
* **Day 5**: Follow up and complete 40+ pro profiles with accurate barangay locations.
* **Day 6**: Test call 10 onboarded pros using the app's SIP/WebRTC call system to ensure CallKeep and VoIP notifications ring reliably on Android/iOS phones.
* **Day 7**: Map check: Ensure every major barangay (Poblacion, Minien, Tebag, Ventinilla, Maningding) has at least 3 active pros.

### Week 2: Demand Ignition (Days 8–14)
> **Goal**: Generate **80–100 real job postings** from Sta. Barbara residents and businesses.

#### 1. Facebook Community Group Strategy
Post in popular local groups:
* *Sta. Barbara Pangasinan Buy & Sell*
* *Calasiao & Dagupan Community & Job Hiring*
* *Urdaneta City Classifieds & Services*
* *Pangasinan Home Improvements & Services*

**Post Copy (Taglish / Authentic Local Tone)**:
> *"Mga taga-Sta. Barbara! May sira bang gripo, bubong, o kailangan magpalinis ng aircon bago mag-init ang panahon?
> 
> May bago tayong app para sa bayan natin: **WorkWorkWork**. Makikita niyo sa map ang mga available na karpintero, tubero, aircon tech, at tagalinis dito lang sa Sta. Barbara at Calasiao.
> 
> 📲 Direktang tawagan sa app nang walang hassle: [App Link]"*

#### 2. Localized Meta (Facebook) Radius Ads (₱250–₱400 / day)
* **Location Targeting**: Santa Barbara, Pangasinan + 8 km radius (covering borders of Calasiao and Urdaneta).
* **Ad Hook**: *"Kailangan mo ba ng mabilisang karpintero, tubero, o aircon cleaning sa Sta. Barbara ngayon?"*
* **Format**: Short 10-second video demo showing: Open map -> Tap nearby pro -> Instant call connected.

#### 3. Seed "Guaranteed Jobs" (The Demand Buffer)
* Post 10–15 real paid tasks (cleaning, yard maintenance, small paint jobs, office tasks) funded by the founding team to ensure the first workers who signed up receive immediate paying gigs within 48 hours.

#### 4. Day-by-Day Checklist (Week 2)
* **Day 8**: Turn on Facebook Ads targeted strictly to Sta. Barbara residents.
* **Day 9–10**: Engage with 10+ local community posts searching for home repairs or service recommendations.
* **Day 11**: Review first job postings and track how quickly pros initiate calls/chats.
* **Day 12–14**: Call clients who posted jobs to ask about their experience (Was the worker responsive? Did the call connect smoothly?).

---

### Week 3: Liquidity Acceleration & Response Optimization (Days 15–21)
> **Goal**: Reach **>75% Match Rate** and bring response time under **10 minutes** across Sta. Barbara.

#### 1. Instant Push Notification Setup
* When a resident in Sta. Barbara posts a job:
  * Push sent instantly to pros in Sta. Barbara, Calasiao, and Urdaneta:
    * *"🚨 Bagong Trabaho sa [Barangay]: [Job Title] - Pindutin para tawagan ang customer."*
* If no pro accepts within 15 minutes, trigger SMS fallback to the top 3 highest-rated local pros in that category.

#### 2. Local Social Proof (Kwento ng Tagumpay)
* Capture 2 short video testimonials / Facebook photo posts:
  1. **Kuya Worker**: *"Dati nag-aabang lang ako sa kanto ng tawag. Ngayon sa WorkWorkWork, may 3 regular akong tawag kada linggo dito sa Minien at Poblacion."*
  2. **Homeowner / Ate**: *"Sobrang bilis magpatawag ng tubero nung pumutok ang tubo namin sa Tebag."*
* Boost these posts with ₱200/day on Facebook for massive local trust.

#### 3. Day-by-Day Checklist (Week 3)
* **Day 15–16**: Review response times. Contact pros who missed calls to troubleshoot notification/battery saver settings on their phones.
* **Day 17–18**: Launch the local video testimonial ads on Facebook and TikTok.
* **Day 19–20**: Trigger in-app review prompts (`react-native-rate`) after completed calls.
* **Day 21**: Verify liquidity scorecard: % of jobs answered within 15 minutes.

---

### Week 4: Referral Engine & Expansion to Calasiao & Urdaneta (Days 22–30)
> **Goal**: Hit **1,000 registered users** in Sta. Barbara corridor and initiate expansion into Calasiao & Urdaneta City.

#### 1. GCash-Friendly Referral Program
* **For Workers**: *"I-share ang iyong Pro Profile sa Messenger. Kapag nag-post o tumawag ang bagong customer gamit ang link mo, may bonus reward ka!"*
* **For Clients**: *"I-refer ang kapitbahay o kaibigan sa Sta. Barbara para sa kanilang home repair needs."*
* In-app referral link: `workworkwork.app/refer/:code`.

#### 2. The 5 Expansion Gate Criteria (Sta. Barbara -> Calasiao / Urdaneta)
Do not expand marketing budget to Calasiao or Urdaneta until Sta. Barbara meets all 5:

| Criterion | Target | Verification in Sta. Barbara |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Match Rate** | **> 75%** of posted jobs get a call/chat within 1 hr | Measured for 7 consecutive days |
| **2. Response Speed** | **< 15 minutes** median first response | Tracked via messaging/call logs |
| **3. Pro Utilization** | **> 40%** of pros get >= 1 gig per week | Validates worker retention |
| **4. Average Rating** | **> 4.6 / 5.0** stars | Ensures high customer satisfaction |
| **5. Organic Word-of-Mouth** | **> 30%** of signups come from shared links | Signals self-sustaining flywheel |

#### 3. Day-by-Day Checklist (Week 4)
* **Day 22–24**: Roll out referral banners and SMS re-engagement.
* **Day 25–27**: Re-activate dormant users with targeted push notifications (*"May 18 verified pros na available sa Sta. Barbara ngayong weekend"*).
* **Day 28–29**: Audit the 5 Gate Criteria for Sta. Barbara.
* **Day 30**: Clone the playbook for **Calasiao (Phase 2)** and **Urdaneta City (Phase 3)**.
